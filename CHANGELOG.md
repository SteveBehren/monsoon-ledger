# Changelog

Corrections and changes to published data are logged here with their date.

Lines can carry tags in brackets after the date. The site reads them: `[correction]` lines are listed on the Corrections page, and `[analysis-01]` lines are listed under "Changes to this paper" on Analysis 01.

New tagline: "AI governance from the archipelago, promises checked against the record." No entry data changed.
- 2026-10-25: Promise outcomes are now computed from deadlines derived by published rules (docs/standard/v0.1), not set by hand. Headline counts now read among dated promises: 4 of 5 dated promises missed and 1 open, with 1 conditional promise and 1 status claim shown apart because they set no deadline. Before, the counts read 4 of 7 deadlines missed and 3 open. The change shows on the home page metric and balance bar, the Promise vs Reality balance and filters, the About abstract, the Method page, the ontology and the RSS feeds. Speaker roles were added for two officials as their sources name them. No promise was added or removed.

No other entry fact changed.
