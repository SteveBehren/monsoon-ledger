# Changelog

Corrections and changes to published data are logged here with their date.

- 2026-10-22: Initial release with four entries and the Janji vs Kenyataan ledger.
- 2026-10-22: Site redesign. New dark console design with a light theme, an interactive safety lens matrix and an instrument map. No data changed.
- 2026-10-22: Added the working-draft legal brief "Silencing by automation" (English and Bahasa Indonesia) and a home page link to it.
- 2026-10-23: Expanded the Method page into a full research protocol and the Instruments page into per-instrument dossiers, both with numbered references. Added a redacted, unverified screenshot to the legal brief. No entry data changed.
- 2026-10-23: Added the monitoring layer. A scheduled scanner checks watched sources twice a week and lists unreviewed leads on a new Radar page. Added the Technical system page under Method. Removed the duplicate instruments table and repeated page headings. No entry data changed.
- 2026-10-23: Added the ontology layer and its explorer, interactive charts and motion across the site, a research abstract on the About page, icons and link preview cards, and a public build log on the Technical system page. New tagline: "AI governance from the archipelago, promises checked against the record." No entry data changed.
- 2026-10-25: Promise outcomes are now computed from deadlines derived by published rules (docs/standard/v0.1), not set by hand. Headline counts now read among dated promises: 4 of 5 dated promises missed and 1 open, with 1 conditional promise and 1 status claim shown apart because they set no deadline. Before, the counts read 4 of 7 deadlines missed and 3 open. The change shows on the home page metric and balance bar, the Promise vs Reality balance and filters, the About abstract, the Method page, the ontology and the RSS feeds. The 3 September 2026 row ("Only the President's signature left") is now a status claim and the 21 October 2026 row (task force once the President signs) is conditional; neither is counted as open any more. Derived deadlines: 13 January 2025 promise, 30 April 2025; 17 June 2025, 30 June 2025; 16 September 2025, 30 September 2025; 16 December 2025, 30 June 2026; 26 January 2026, 31 December 2026.
- 2026-10-25: The one-line outcome text of the five dated promises now says what happened (for example "Still a draft as of 21 October 2026") instead of repeating the word Missed or Open, since the outcome itself is computed. Speaker roles were added for two officials as their sources name them. No promise was added or removed.
- 2026-10-25: Correction to the entry "A 72-hour breach clock, and nobody to call": a failure to notify a breach falls in the GDPR's lower fine tier, up to 10 million euros or 2% of worldwide annual turnover (Article 83(4)(a)), not the 4% tier, which covers infringements such as the basic principles and data subjects' rights (Article 83(5)). No other entry fact changed.
- 2026-10-25: Added a receipt page and share card for every promise, the published promise format with its schema and a kit for other countries, and preserved copies of sources in the Internet Archive. Removed the home page weather map and the running clock, which repeated the same seven deadlines; the oldest missed promise now leads the home and ledger pages.
