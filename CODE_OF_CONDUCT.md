# Code of conduct

Monsoon Ledger is a public project about public institutions. Disagreement about facts, sources and readings of the law is welcome. Personal attacks are not.

## Expected

- Argue from sources. Link what you rely on.
- Be careful with people's names and roles. Officials are discussed for what they said on the record, never for their private lives.
- Write plainly and assume good faith, in English or Bahasa Indonesia.
- Accept corrections, including corrections to your own contributions.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or threats, in public or in private.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Mocking people, groups, languages or countries.
- Deliberately misleading claims, or sources presented as something they are not.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@SteveBehren](https://github.com/SteveBehren)) may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues, and block accounts that break this code. Report a problem by contacting the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
